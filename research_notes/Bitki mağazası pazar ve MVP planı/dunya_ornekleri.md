# Global online houseplant stores and plant apps: reference models for a plant e-commerce app MVP

Research date: 2026-10-25. Every claim has an inline source. Dates are marked. "(older)" means the source is from before 2025 and the figure may be out of date. Several company sites (thesill.com, Shopify-hosted shops) returned HTTP 429/403 to direct fetching, so some policy details come from search-engine snippets of the official pages; this is noted where it applies.

## 1. Who are the notable players, how do they operate, and which have closed, been acquired or pivoted?

### Takeaway
The houseplant D2C wave of 2012-2020 has consolidated. Patch (UK) was folded into Arena Flowers (Jan 2023). Bloombox Club was sold to PlantX for £8m (Dec 2020). Bergamotte (FR) was bought by Bloom & Wild (Jul 2021). Horti (US subscription) closed by choice on 31 May 2025. Rooted NYC is listed as closed. Bakker.com went bankrupt in 2018 and was relaunched. The independents still standing (The Sill, Bloomscape, Léon & George, PLNTS, Plantsome, Ugaoo) are mostly small or medium-sized, and the best-documented one, The Sill, lost about $6.7m on $13.4m revenue in 2022. The plant apps (PictureThis, Planta, Greg) are far bigger by user count than any store. Greg is the only major care app that has added its own plant shop.

### Inferences
- The well-funded US D2C stores (The Sill about $20M+ raised, Bloomscape $24M) peaked during the pandemic. Afterwards they cut burn, added categories (outdoor) and opened physical stores rather than raising large new rounds. This pattern is consistent with live-plant D2C having thin margins (about 38% gross at The Sill, before shipping and replacements).
- The common exit is being acquired by a flower or gifting company (Patch to Arena, Bergamotte to Bloom & Wild), which shows the synergy in shared perishable logistics and gifting demand.
- No major store has a strong standalone shopping app; Patch's site mentions none. Greg, which went app-first and then added a shop, is the closest model to a "plant store app". The care-app audiences (PictureThis 1.1M ratings, Planta 114K) dwarf any store's customer base.

## 2. Business models: D2C vs marketplace vs subscription vs gifting. Which are sustainable?

### Takeaway
Single-brand D2C with gifting and B2B add-ons survives, but at modest scale and usually after cost cuts. Pure plant subscriptions have the weakest track record. Horti closed in 2025. Bloombox was sold in 2020 to a parent now under financial strain. B&D and Bloombox keep subscriptions only as one product among many. The healthiest growth story in the brief is omnichannel: Ugaoo combines its own site, marketplaces (Amazon, Flipkart) and planned retail stores.

### Cited Findings
- The Sill is D2C, with about 75% of sales from its website, plus 4 physical stores. It expanded into outdoor plants in 2024 to triple its addressable market. — [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/the-sill-expands-outdoor-plants-gardening-products/710662)
- The Sill's losses ($6.9M in 2021, $6.7M in 2022) narrowed toward breakeven only by reducing burn, which also shrank revenue. — [Wefunder via search](https://wefunder.com/thesill/details); [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/operations/the-sill-launches-a-new-crowdfunding-campaign-to-extend-runway/)
- At Léon & George, B2B office plants were about 20% of revenue, almost all inbound. — [Kingscrowd](https://kingscrowd.com/founder-profile-leon-george/). Bloomscape offers corporate gifts and gifts by occasion (Birthday, Housewarming, Teacher, Sympathy, Plant Newbie), plus e-gift cards. — [Bloomscape](https://bloomscape.com/shop/plants/)
- Patch's About page lists "business plants" and gifts as categories. — [Patch About](https://patchplants.com/gb/en/about). It was acquired by a flower and gift group whose CEO wanted Patch's brand-marketing skills. — [Hortweek via search](https://www.hortweek.com/arena-takes-online-plant-retailer-patch-plants/retail/article/1810005)

- Ugaoo sells through its own site plus Amazon and Flipkart, and is adding retail stores. Revenue went from ₹24 Cr to ₹63 Cr between FY2023 and FY2024. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/gardening-startup-ugaoo-bags-inr-47-cr-led-by-v3-ventures/)

- The app model (freemium subscription) is clearly the larger revenue pool. ### Inferences
- For a new MVP, the safest model is a curated single-store D2C catalog with gifting built in (gift message, occasion collections, gift cards), with B2B or office orders as an inbound side channel. A subscription box should wait until a repeat-purchase base exists.
- Being close to a grower (an own nursery or a few fixed grower partners) seems to be a structural advantage over dropship-only models, given the margins.
- Selling on a marketplace alongside the own app (the Ugaoo pattern) can widen reach in markets where marketplaces dominate (India, SE Asia, and likely Turkey with Trendyol and Hepsiburada; an inference, not researched).

### Gaps
- No public unit-economics data per order (contribution margin after shipping and replacements) was found for any player.
- No reliable subscription churn data for Horti, Bloombox or B&D was found.

## 3. Catalog and product structure: how products are modeled

### Takeaway
The leading stores model a product as **plant species, then size tier, then pot (style or material, then colour)**, often sold as a plant + pot bundle, with the pot also sold separately. Each plant is tagged with **light need, pet-safety, difficulty/care level, air-purifying, and price band**, and these tags power the filters. Gifts are organised by occasion collections. This maps cleanly onto a catalog of plants, categories, colors, pot types, plant types, banners, promo codes and reviews. The fields usually missing from such a schema are a **size dimension (with height ranges)**, **care attributes** (light, water, difficulty, pet-safe) and **occasion/gift collections**.

### Cited Findings
- **Bloomscape filter model (live, Oct 2026):**
  - Indoor light: Low/Artificial, Partial/Bright Indirect, Direct.
  - Plant size tiers with height ranges: XS 5-12", SM 7-18", MD 1-2 ft, LG 1.5-2.5 ft, XL 2-3 ft, XXL 3-5 ft, plus Bundles.
  - Difficulty: No-Fuss, Easy, Moderate.
  - Pet-friendly: Yes/No. Air-cleaner: Yes/No.
  - Price bands: <$50, $50-100, $100-150, $150-200, $200+.
  - Pot colour: about 15 values (black, charcoal, clay, slate, stone, white and others).
  - Sort by featured, price or newest.
  - Example prices: Snake Plant $24 (sale $20.40); Spider Plant MD $69; Burgundy Rubber Tree LG $99; Fiddle Leaf Fig Tree $600 (sale $480); Ecopots round pot 10" sold separately for $40.
  - [Bloomscape shop](https://bloomscape.com/shop/plants/)

- "Colors" in the planned schema should probably attach to pot or variant, not to plant (plant colour, such as variegation, is a separate optional attribute).

## 4. Key app and site features

### Takeaway
Table stakes on the store side are care info per plant, attribute filters (light, pet-safe, difficulty, size, price), a 30-day guarantee with a photo-based claim flow, gifting and gift cards, promotions/sales, and rewards in some cases. Speed differs by market: the UK offers next-day and chosen-day delivery 7 days a week through DPD, while US D2C typically takes about 3-8 days to ship plus 3-5 days in transit.

## 6. What drives retention and repeat purchases?

### Takeaway
Players consistently point to ongoing **care support after the sale** (plant doctors, care guides, care apps, reminders) as the retention lever, because plant buyers "have a lot more questions" and dead plants end repeat buying. Beginner-friendly education also lowers acquisition cost. No player publishes a repeat-purchase rate or customer lifetime value.

### Cited Findings
- Bloomscape bought Vera so customers would stay invested in the plants they buy. Founder Justin Mast said plant buyers "have a lot more questions" than buyers of other household goods. — [Modern Retail podcast, 2020](https://www.modernretail.co/retailers/bloomscape-founder-justin-mast-shipping-living-things-acquired-plant-care-app-vera-series-b/)
- Patch's work with beginner plant buyers (including a "plant parenting course") cut CAC by 20% and built a loyal buyer base, per its marketing agency. — [Mayple case study](https://www.mayple.com/case-studies/patch-plants)
- The Patch Promise of lifetime plant advice, plant doctors and the Patch Perks rewards programme. — [Patch About](https://patchplants.com/gb/en/about); [The Design Sheppard interview](https://www.thedesignsheppard.com/interviews/interview-freddie-blackett-founder-of-patch)
- Horti's founders described their community as the core asset they would not sell. — [Horti closure notice (via search)](https://heyhorti.com/pages/help-center-faqs)
- Greg rewards UGC (unboxing videos earn a $5 credit) and ties free shipping and lifetime replacement to its subscription. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/greg-plant-identifier-care/id1512912236)
- Bloombox Club gives longer subscription plans store vouchers of up to £50. — [Bloombox](https://bloomboxclub.com/products/plant-subscription-box)
- The Sill relies on seasonal sales events, trend content and gift plants (orchids +51% YoY). — [Bob Vila](https://www.bobvila.com/reviews/the-sill-spring-plant-sale-april-2026/); [Apartment Therapy](https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/the-sill-plant-trend-report-37521215)

### Inferences
- The cheapest retention mechanic for an MVP is automatic "my plants" care reminders for every plant bought. The species is known from the order, so no identification feature is needed. Push notifications can then carry seasonal promos and "next plant" recommendations.
- A loyalty or credit system (points, review credits, referral credit) and gift cards are cheap to add and appear across players. Rewards and UGC credit look like phase 2 features.

### Gaps

## 7. MVP lessons: table stakes at launch vs differentiators added later

### Takeaway
At launch, competitors had a curated catalog with size and pot variants, care-attribute filters, per-plant care info, clear shipping rules with a free-shipping threshold, a 30-day photo-based guarantee, and gifting. Everything heavier came years later or through acquisition: care apps, plant doctors, subscriptions, rewards, B2B, physical stores and outdoor ranges.

### Cited Findings
- **Timeline evidence:**
  - The Sill launched online in 2012 and added outdoor plants only in Mar 2024. — [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/the-sill-expands-outdoor-plants-gardening-products/710662)
  - Bloomscape added care-app features by acquiring Vera in Sep 2020, after the store was established. — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200930005620/en/Bloomscape-Raises-%2415M-in-Series-B-Funding-After-Major-Year-of-Growth)
  - Patch's plant-doctor service was announced as a new launch in a later interview. — [SupplyCompass](https://supplycompass.com/blog/feature/inconversationwith/patch/)
  - Greg launched in Oct 2020 as a care app with a free tier limited to 5 plants, and the shop came later. — [TechCrunch 2021](https://techcrunch.com/2021/05/27/greg-an-app-for-plant-lovers-grows-5-4-million-in-seed-funding); [App Store 2026](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/greg-plant-identifier-care/id1512912236)
- **Guarantee and shipping rules are universal:** 30 days at Patch, The Sill, Bloomscape and B&D; 3 months at Plantsome. — [Patch](https://patchplants.com/gb/en/delivery); [The Sill FAQ](https://www.thesill.com/pages/faq); [Bloomscape](https://bloomscape.com/bloomscape-guarantee/); [B&D](https://www.beardsanddaisies.co.uk/products/houseplant-subscription); [Plantsome](https://www.plantsome.nl/pages/shop)
- **Filters on light, size, difficulty, pet-safety and price are live at Bloomscape.** — [Bloomscape](https://bloomscape.com/shop/plants/)
- **Subscriptions are risky as a core model:** Horti closed in 2025 ([Horti](https://heyhorti.com/pages/help-center-faqs)); Bloombox Club's parent sold assets to repay a secured loan in 2024 ([PlantX filing](https://sedar-filings-primary.thecse.com/00032344/2408300616565843.pdf)).
- **Capital efficiency matters:** The Sill grew to $16.5M revenue on about $20M raised but lost about $6.7-6.9M a year in 2021-2022 before cutting to near breakeven. — [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/operations/the-sill-launches-a-new-crowdfunding-campaign-to-extend-runway/). Léon & George ran at about $1.9M revenue with a 10-person team. — [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/leon--george)
- Care apps show users want identification, diagnosis and reminders, but they monetise through subscriptions, and users complain about paywalls. — [Kimola (Greg)](https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-key-insights-greg-app-customer-feedback-report-app-store-us-155864); [Planta](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/planta-ai-plant-garden-care/id1410126781)

### Inferences

**MVP must-haves (table stakes)**
1. Catalog: species with care attributes (light, water, difficulty, pet-safe, air-purifying), size tiers with heights, pot type and colour variants, bundles, and stand-alone pots.
2. Filters and search on those attributes, plus category, plant type, price and colour.
3. A product detail page with photos, size and height info and a care card.
4. Cart and checkout with promo codes and a gift message or gift option.
5. Delivery rules: fee, free threshold, minimum order, delivery dates or slots, and a weather-hold flag.
6. Order tracking and notifications.
7. A 30-day guarantee claim flow (photo upload, then replacement or refund).
8. Reviews, a wishlist, and banners/campaigns on the home screen.
9. Basic care guide content.

**Phase 2 (differentiators that came later)**
- "My Plants" care reminders for purchased plants.
- Loyalty or points and referral or UGC credits.
- Gift cards and occasion collections.
- Plant-doctor chat or consultation.
- Identification and diagnosis (via a third-party API).
- Light meter.
- Community.
- Subscription box.
- B2B or office orders.
- Marketplace channels, physical stores, outdoor ranges.

**Avoid at MVP**
- Building identification or diagnosis AI in-house. PictureThis, Planta and Greg are entrenched with millions of users.
- Subscription-first economics.
- A very wide catalog.

### Gaps
- No direct statement from a founder listing "what we launched with" was found. The timeline above is reconstructed from dated announcements.
- No benchmark conversion-rate or AOV data was found for plant apps versus web stores.
